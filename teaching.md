If you are interested in studying sound art, music, or music technology, I teach a range of evening classes at Morley College, London and also offer online private tuition. I have over a decade of experience teaching music and sound to students ranging from complete beginners to PhD level. 

# Evening Classes - London
During term time, I teach several evening classes for adults at Morley College, London. Many classes are suitable for beginners, with progression available onto advanced classes. Current subject areas include:
* [Sound Art](https://www.morleycollege.ac.uk/courses?s=sound%20art){:target="_blank"} - Monday and Thursday evenings
* [Experimental Music Performance](https://www.morleycollege.ac.uk/courses?s=experimental%20music){:target="_blank"} - Wednesday evenings
* [Mixing and Mastering](https://www.morleycollege.ac.uk/courses?s=mixing%20mastering){:target="_blank"} - Thursday evenings

More information and enrolment details can be found on the [Morley College website](https://www.morleycollege.ac.uk){:target="_blank"}.

# Private Tuition - Online
If you are looking for something tailored to your interests or are unable to attend classes in London, I offer online one-to-one tuition. 

Sessions can focus on technical skills in music and sound technology; project development; music composition; or broader artist development. Lessons are structured to your experience level and goals and are suitable for both beginners and established practiioners. 

Private tuition is £40 per hour with discounts for longer sessions and block bookings. 

To enquire, please get in touch via the [contact form](/contact) with a brief outline of your background and what you would like to work on.
